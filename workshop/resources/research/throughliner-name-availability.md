# Name availability — "Throughline" vs "Throughliner"

Searched 2026-07-30 while choosing a replacement name for "Sovereign Implementer". The rename direction was to move away from the overclaiming "Sovereign" framing toward the tool's real essence — durable memory of *reasoning* (the why-pipeline), a "throughline" of rationale that survives a forgetful session.

## "Throughline" — crowded, and one competitor is itself software

- **[getthroughline.com](https://www.getthroughline.com/)** — a card-based story-writing **software app** for macOS/Windows. Closest clash: another software product with the exact name.
- **[throughlinecare.com](https://www.throughlinecare.com/)** — ThroughLine, a mental-health crisis-support platform used by Trust & Safety teams (Find A Helpline). Well-known in its space.
- **[Throughline, Inc.](https://www.linkedin.com/company/throughlineinc)** — a Washington, DC enterprise design/strategy & comms firm, founded 2005.

Conclusion: the bare word "Throughline" is taken across multiple entities, including a software product. A bare-name adoption would collide.

## "Throughline for Claude Code" — rejected

Qualifying with the platform disambiguates from the competitors, but bakes in "Claude Code" — it would be wrong the moment a future port to another platform happens. The name must be **port-resilient**, so a platform qualifier is out.

## "Throughliner" — clear

Searched `"Throughliner" software OR app OR product OR company` (2026-07-30). No software, app, or company named "Throughliner". Only hits were the existing *Throughline* products above and unrelated tools (Streamliner, OmniOutliner). Coining the `-r` suffix into a single distinct word does the disambiguating work: it is not "Throughline + descriptor" (which still leans on the clashing token), it is a new word — trademark-distinct, platform-neutral, and still carrying the throughline/rationale essence ("the thing that keeps your throughline").

**Decision (2026-07-30):** rename to **Throughliner**. Slug/repo `throughliner` (namespaced under the marketplace/org, so no collision with the external products). Confirm the repo/slug is actually free before committing.
