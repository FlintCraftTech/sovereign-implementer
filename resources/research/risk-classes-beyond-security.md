# Risk classes beyond security — what the record's misses actually were

Compiled 2026-08-30, on the spot in the planning session that asked, from this
project's own record plus one instance from another of the user's projects,
held in scrubbed general form at her direction. The question: does the red-flag
screen (security, privacy, breach) watch for enough risk types, or a broad
enough risk to contain more?

## The instances, each classified

1. **The scrubbed instance (another project).** Research was done; whether it
   applied to the actual situation was never checked or flagged; no verifying
   follow-up was suggested; the gap cost months of effort and damaged a
   working relationship. Class: **unverified premise under sustained effort**.
   **Corrected 2026-08-30 by the user, and the correction sharpens the class:**
   the premise did not go stale — it was never valid, because the research
   answered the questions asked without ever asking the higher one, in her
   words: *"the user's questions notwithstanding, is the approach itself valid
   for the given situation?"* So the sub-class is **mis-scoped research** —
   research correct in its answers and wrong in its frame — and a
   staleness-shaped fix (re-verify later) would not have caught it, since
   there was nothing true to go stale. Her candidate criteria for what
   research must assess are filed as [research-validity-criteria].
2. **The falsified announcement (this project).** A public claim was falsified
   on 2026-08-27 and stood for a week; the register's own status line was
   wrong, so the repeal-grep never fired. Class: **record–world divergence**
   (a record asserting something false about the world), compounding into
   **public-claim staleness**.
3. **The comparison article's stale research.** A two-month-old research file
   claimed a competitor capability was absent; it had shipped. Caught only by
   a mandated re-verify step written into the item. Class: **research
   staleness** — a premise that was true when filed.
4. **The zip separators.** Every shipped zip non-conformant for weeks because
   "works on this machine" was generalised to "works"; unknown whether anyone
   was harmed. Class: **environment generalisation** — a sub-case of
   unverified premise.
5. **The cycle observable (2026-08-30).** A due-ness check read the cycle's
   authoring record as a completed turn and reported falsely; caught the same
   day. Class: **mechanism self-deception** — a check whose clean result does
   not mean what it appears to mean.
6. **The untracked sent register.** The one unrecoverable project file had no
   deletion guard until someone noticed. Class: **irrecoverable-artifact
   exposure**.

## The map

```
covered by the screen today   security · privacy · breach (class kept narrow
                              on purpose; nothing here argues for blurring it)
machinery built AFTER a miss  public-claim staleness (the sweep cycle) ·
                              record–world divergence (case-by-case fixes) ·
                              irrecoverable artifacts (the register guard)
NO machinery, and the         UNVERIFIED PREMISE UNDER SUSTAINED EFFORT —
  costliest class on record   instances 1, 3, 4. Every nearby mechanism (the
                              what-would-answer-this trigger, the
                              capability-claim rule, the Superseded-by flag)
                              fires when a FACT IS WRITTEN. None fires when
                              EFFORT IS SPENT on a premise nobody re-checked.
```

## The finding

The screen does not need more types of the kind it has — it needs one sibling
of a different kind. Security risk is about what a change could expose;
premise risk is about what continued work could waste. Its signature is that
the cost accrues silently while effort continues, so the natural checkpoints
(a write, a send, a commit) never see it. The moments that could see it are
the ones that survey effort: the planning session's revisit of long-lived
work, and the moment an item's scope or duration grows.

**The honest limit, stated because it binds any rule this produces:** this
would be a noticing duty of the security screen's class — it improves the
odds and guarantees nothing, catches only what is in view, and must say so
wherever it is written.

## What this does NOT conclude

No rule wording is chosen here, and no screen widening is decided — that is
the rule gate's job at a planning turn, on a capture citing this file. The
companion research (eliciting-proactive-research-offers.md) tests candidate
wordings; the two were deliberately kept separate so evidence and rule-craft
do not blur.
