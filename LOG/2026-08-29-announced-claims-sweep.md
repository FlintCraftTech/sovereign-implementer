# 7b751b6 — plan — a weekly sweep over announced claims, scoped to what pruning cannot reach

Authored after the user spotted the hole in correcting posts only as they arise: *"I don't know how that would work without rendering old things wrong as they're not checked because they don't 'arise'."* She was right, and today proved it — the 2026-08-22 announcement was falsified on the 27th and nothing fired for a week.

**Cadence weekly, declared by her**, with her own derivation: a release is the thing most likely to falsify a public claim, so the sweep runs at the rhythm of the thing that breaks its subject.

**Its scope is deliberately partial, and the definition says so.** Channels that *return* their pruned posts re-check themselves — a pruned post comes back as a capture and its claims are verified before reposting ([channel-depth-and-recycling]). Channels that *retire* get no such pass, and that is what this covers.

**Widened the same session, on her instruction, to cover the forums as well** — the how-to forum and the ports forum both hold standing instructional posts whose claims go stale exactly as an announcement's do, and their lines are already in `INBOX/sent.md`, so the first step reaches them unchanged. **A fourth cycle was considered and refused:** one definition covering every published claim beats two that can drift apart.

**Forums need a second check channels do not** — ordering. A forum lists topics by latest activity, so posting shuffles them out of their numbered sequence; observed the same day with the how-to topics displaying 3, 6, 5, 4, 2. Report only, since re-ordering is the user's and whether it can be done at all is one of the open questions.

**Two limits written into the definition:** its observable is a record written by the same session that ran the turn, so it proves a sweep happened and not that it was thorough — unlike the release cycle's external publication date. And its cost grows with the register.

**Queue changes:** [announced-claims-sweep] cycle authored in `CYCLES.md`, then widened to the forums.
**Work processed:** none — a cycle definition, not a queue item.
